    🍗 Protein: The Builder
    Protein keeps your muscles strong and stops you feeling hungry. Try to include a source of protein with every meal.
      🥣 Fibre: The Digestive Helper
      Soluble Fibre dissolves in water (like oats and beans). It keeps you full and helps lower cholesterol.

      Insoluble Fibre doesn't dissolve (like whole grains and nuts). It keeps things moving through your gut.
